### Phase 1: The Temperature Switch (Level: Apply)

**Goal:** Make the `micro:bit` speak and tell you if it is "Cold" or "Not Cold" when you press Button A.

* **The Concept:** We use a "threshold" (a limit number). For example, if the temperature is below **18°C**, it is cold.
* **The Logic:** * **IF** the temperature is less than 18  Speak "This is cold!"
* **ELSE** (if it is 18 or higher)  Speak "It is not cold."
